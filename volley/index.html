Volley Pinball Restoration

A restoration of a 1976 Gottlieb Volley pinball machine

About

This restoration project documents the refurbishment of a 1976 Gottlieb Volley pinball machine from 2011 to 2015.

The project included playfield restoration, cabinet repair, pop bumper repair, and cosmetic improvements.
